Care Guide

Wipe your coffee table regularly with a soft, dry cloth, using a lightly dampened one only when needed. Keep it away from direct sunlight, heat, and moisture to prevent fading or warping. Use coasters or mats under hot or cold items to protect the surface, and avoid dragging objects across it. Check fittings occasionally to ensure everything stays stable and secure.

Everything You Need To Know

Why is mango wood a great choice for a coffee table?

Mango wood is durable, naturally beautiful, and features unique grain patterns that make every table one of a kind.

Do coffee tables with metal legs last longer?

Yes, metal legs provide excellent stability, durability, and add a sleek modern-industrial touch.

Which décor styles suit a wood and metal coffee table?

It blends beautifully with industrial, contemporary, rustic, coastal, and modern interiors.

How do I style a wooden coffee table?

Keep it simple with a decorative tray, coffee table books, candles, or a small plant for a balanced look.

Can a round coffee table make a living room feel more spacious?

Yes, its curved shape improves movement and creates a more open, welcoming layout, especially in compact spaces.

What should you look for in a coffee table for daily use?

Look for a sturdy construction, practical proportions, and a surface that is easy to maintain.

Can a wooden coffee table soften an industrial-style room?

Yes, natural wood adds warmth and texture that can balance metal, concrete, and other harder finishes.

Should a coffee table be lighter or darker than the sofa?

Either can work. A contrasting tone can add depth, while similar shades create a more seamless look.

How do you choose a coffee table for an open-plan living room?

Consider the seating arrangement, walking space, and surrounding furniture so the table feels proportionate to the room.

What can you keep on a coffee table without making it look cluttered?

A small tray, a few books, or one decorative object can provide function and style while keeping the surface tidy.
