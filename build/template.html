Care & Buying Guide

Care Guide

Dust your dining table regularly with a soft, dry cloth, using a lightly dampened one only when needed. Keep it away from direct sunlight, heat, and moisture to prevent fading or warping. Always use coasters, placemats, and trivets under hot or cold items to protect the surface, and lift décor rather than dragging it. Check fittings occasionally to keep the table steady and secure.

Buying Guide

Our dining tables often feature natural materials like wood, stone, or marble, so subtle variations in grain, colour, and texture are completely normal. Colours may also appear slightly different depending on lighting or screen settings. These natural differences aren’t flaws. They’re what make each FableRoom piece feel unique, crafted with care, and made to last.
