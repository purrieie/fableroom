Care Guide

Dust your cabinet regularly with a soft, dry cloth, and use a lightly dampened one only when needed. Keep it away from excess moisture, direct sunlight, and heat to prevent warping or fading. Always lift objects rather than dragging them, and check fittings from time to time to ensure everything stays stable and secure. If storing, make sure the cabinet is fully dry and follow any recommended protective treatments.

FAQ

Everything You Need To Know

We work directly with skilled makers who maintain fair wages and follow sustainable practices, cutting out middlemen. This lets us offer at least 30% savings on most of our products, and often more.

Every piece is sourced from carefully vetted makers using premium materials and traditional techniques. Many of our artisans have been perfecting their craft for generations, creating pieces built to last - not just look good in photos.

Every product is also backed by our 365-Day Guarantee: any verifiable manufacturing or material fault within a year is repaired, replaced, or refunded - your choice. This covers manufacturing and material faults, not accidental damage or normal wear.

Many of our pieces are finished by hand, which means natural variation in colour, grain, or texture is normal and expected - no two pieces are ever perfectly identical. This is part of what makes handcrafted items unique, not a fault. Check the individual product page for details on that item's specific finish.

Most of our products are handcrafted by skilled artisans in India, using traditional techniques passed down through generations, while a few collections are sourced from Vietnam.

We don't operate physical showrooms - this keeps our costs low so we can pass those savings on to you. If a piece doesn't work in your space, our 14-day return policy has you covered.

All orders are prepaid at checkout - we don't offer cash on delivery. Your payment is taken when your order is confirmed, and the price shown at checkout is the price you pay.

You can request a cancellation within 14 days of your order confirmation, provided it hasn't shipped yet - once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled or modified, even within that window. After either point, your item will already be with our makers and we're unable to process a cancellation. To cancel, email us at support@fableroom.com with your order number and we'll take care of the rest.

Delivery time depends on availability: Ready to Ship arrives in 1-2 weeks, Standard Shipping in 3-8 weeks, and Pre-Order in 8-12 weeks - Astra is currently Pre-Order.

We deliver throughout England, Scotland, and Wales. For Northern Ireland, we offer delivery of smaller items with a minimal surcharge.
